Privacy Policy
Effective Date: May 14, 2026
1. Introduction
Welcome to NuraMatch. NuraMatch ("we", "our", or "us") is a peer support platform that connects people going through similar mental health experiences so they can support each other. We are based in Toronto, Ontario, Canada.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information when you use the NuraMatch mobile application and related services (collectively, the "Services").

By using NuraMatch, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our Services.

This policy complies with the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), Canada's federal privacy law, and takes into account the requirements of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for users in the European Union and United Kingdom.
2. Information We Collect
We collect the following categories of personal information directly from you:

2.1 Identity Information
Display name or username chosen by you
Email address
Profile photo (optional)
Age (visible only to you and never shared with other users or third parties)

2.2 Mental Health and Wellbeing Information
This is our most sensitive category of information. We collect it solely to provide you with appropriate peer support matching and a personalized experience:
Daily mood check-ins including your selected emotional state (okay, struggling, or in crisis)
Emotional tags and feelings you select during check-ins
Struggle categories and mental health topic tags you identify with
Coping strategies you select
Journal entries and private notes you create in the Reflect tab
Mood history and trend data accumulated over time
Optional notes added during check-ins

2.3 Connection and Support Preferences
Type of support you are looking for
Communication style preferences
Profile prompt answers you choose to complete
Whether you prefer to support others or receive support

2.4 Account and Usage Information
Login method (Google OAuth or email)
Check-in streak and badge history
Saved affirmations
Anonymous or named profile status
App usage patterns and engagement data

2.5 Technical Information
Device type and operating system
App version
General timezone for matching purposes
Crash reports and error logs
3. How We Use Your Information
We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

3.1 Providing the Service
Matching you with appropriate peer support connections based on your mood, struggles, and preferences
Enforcing matching safety rules including ensuring crisis users are only matched with stable users
Powering the AI companion chat when no human match is available
Displaying your mood trends and journal history in the Reflect tab
Sending push notifications about new matches and messages

3.2 Safety and Moderation
Monitoring conversations using AI to detect grooming language, manipulation, and escalating distress
Calculating internal trust scores to identify and restrict bad actors
Reviewing flagged conversations through human moderators when safety concerns are detected
Surfacing crisis resources when distress signals are identified

3.3 Improving the Service
Analyzing aggregated and anonymized platform usage to improve matching quality
Monitoring platform health metrics including check-in completion rates and match success rates
Fixing bugs and improving app performance
4. Mental Health Data Protection
NuraMatch collects sensitive mental health information. We treat this information with the highest level of care and protection. We make the following commitments:

Your mental health data is never sold to third parties under any circumstances
Your mental health data is never used for advertising or marketing purposes
Private journal entries and personal notes are never accessed by NuraMatch staff or administrators
Your mental health data is encrypted both in transit and at rest
You may request complete deletion of all your mental health data at any time by contacting privacy@nuramatch.com
Crisis-related information shared within the app is used solely to connect you with appropriate support resources and is never shared externally without your explicit consent except where required by applicable law in situations involving imminent risk to life

We do not require you to disclose your gender identity, race, ethnicity, or legal name. These are not collected by NuraMatch.
5. How We Share Your Information
We do not sell your personal information. We share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

5.1 With Your Match
When you are matched with another user, limited profile information is shared with them including your display name, profile photo if provided, bio, topic tags, and prompt answers. Your age, email address, and mental health check-in data are never shared with your match.

5.2 Service Providers
We work with trusted third party service providers who help us operate NuraMatch. These providers are contractually obligated to protect your information and may only use it to provide services to us:
OpenAI: Powers the AI companion chat and content moderation features
Google Firebase: Provides authentication, push notifications, and real-time messaging infrastructure
Amazon Web Services (AWS): Provides cloud hosting and infrastructure
MongoDB Atlas: Provides database services
Resend: Provides email delivery services

5.3 Legal Requirements
We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities including to meet national security or law enforcement requirements.

5.4 Safety and Crisis Situations
In situations involving imminent risk to life, we may share limited information with emergency services or crisis intervention resources where required by applicable Canadian law.
6. Data Retention
We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with our Services. Specifically:

Account information is retained until you delete your account
Mood check-in history is retained for the duration of your account to enable trend analysis in the Reflect tab
Journal entries and private notes are retained until you delete them or delete your account
Conversation history with matched users is deleted when either user removes the connection
Trust score data is retained for platform safety purposes for up to 12 months after account deletion
Anonymized and aggregated analytics data may be retained indefinitely as it cannot identify you

Upon account deletion, all personal information including mental health data is permanently deleted within 30 days except where retention is required by law.
7. International Data Transfers
NuraMatch operates from Canada. Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your country of residence, including the United States, where our service providers operate.

When transferring personal data internationally, NuraMatch relies on Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and uses service providers who maintain their own GDPR and international data transfer compliance frameworks, including Google Firebase, OpenAI, and AWS.

Canada has received an adequacy decision from the European Commission, meaning that data transferred from the EU to Canada is considered to have adequate protection under PIPEDA.

9. Security
We take the security of your personal information seriously, particularly given the sensitive nature of mental health data. Our security measures include:

Encryption of all personal data in transit using TLS
Encryption of sensitive data at rest
Secure authentication including OAuth 2.0 and email verification
Access controls limiting who can access personal information
Regular security monitoring and incident response procedures
AI and human moderation to protect users within the platform

While we implement these safeguards, no security system is impenetrable. In the event of a data breach that poses a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify you and relevant authorities as required by applicable law.
10. Age Requirements
NuraMatch is intended for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 18. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a person under 18, we will take steps to delete that information as quickly as possible.

If you believe we may have collected information from someone under 18, please contact us immediately at privacy@nuramatch.com.
11. AI and Automated Processing
NuraMatch uses artificial intelligence and automated processing in the following ways:

Matching algorithm: An automated system analyzes your mood check-ins, trajectory data, and preferences to suggest peer matches. This system operates under strict safety rules enforced at the database level.
AI companion chat: An AI powered by OpenAI's language models provides emotional support when no human match is available. This AI is instructed to follow safe messaging guidelines and route users to crisis resources when appropriate.
Content moderation: AI systems monitor conversations in real time to detect harmful language, grooming patterns, and escalating distress. Flagged conversations may be reviewed by human moderators.
Trust scoring: An internal behavioral scoring system silently tracks reports, blocks, and conversation tone to identify bad actors. This system is not visible to users.

You have the right to request human review of any significant automated decision that affects you by contacting privacy@nuramatch.com.
12. Human Moderation and Conversation Review
By using NuraMatch you consent to the possibility that flagged conversations may be reviewed by trained human moderators. This review occurs only when our AI systems detect potential safety concerns and is conducted solely for the purpose of protecting users.

We are transparent about this practice because for a mental health app, knowing a real human can step in if something goes seriously wrong is a feature designed to protect you, not a surveillance concern.

All moderators are bound by strict confidentiality obligations and may only use conversation content for the purpose of safety review.
